Simple Ways to Reduce Your Footprint

Small everyday changes can help you build more sustainable habits.

  • 🚲 Walk, cycle, or use public transportation when practical.
  • 💡 Switch off lights and appliances when they are not needed.
  • 🌳 Plant and protect trees in your community.
  • ♻️ Reuse items and recycle suitable materials.
  • 🥗 Reduce food waste and consider more plant-based meals.
  • 🛍️ Choose durable products and avoid unnecessary purchases.
